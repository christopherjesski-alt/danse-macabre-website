¶The Team

The cast and crew bringing 1348 to life.

Ethan Hewlett

Writer and Director

During my time at Emerson, I have specialized in telling stories through movement and fight. From fight direction in theater, to stunt performance and choreography in student films, and directing dance music videos, I’ve developed a passion for telling stories of narrative violence and physicality.

From the director’s note

¶Dramatis Personae

Isabel

Grace Wagner

On screen, Grace has played Kateri in A Salt Weapon (2026) and the Stage Manager in Looking at Madness (2024), and doubled stunts for Joyce in Human First (2026). On stage she has appeared in Henry V at Chicago Shakespeare Theater and in Channeling Our Ancestors at the Tulsa Performing Arts Center.

Guillaume

Robert Najarian

A stage and screen actor and fight choreographer, Robert was an original US cast member of Punchdrunk’s Sleep No More and has appeared in Disney+’s Godmothered. A Fight Director with the Society of American Fight Directors, he works in historical European weapons including the German longsword, and is the author of The Art of Unarmed Stage Combat.

Father Tobias

Robert Walsh

A member of SAG-AFTRA, Actors’ Equity, SDC and SAFD, Robert has played principal and featured roles in Black Mass, Amistad, State and Main, The Spanish Prisoner, Eight Men Out, Evening, Dead Reckoning, Fourth of July and Love on the Vineyard.
